## 🤔 Why the Codeit AI Engineer Track?

I'm a computer science major with a deep interest in training and biomechanics, and what I want is a world where everyone can train hard without getting hurt. What draws me most is technology like the OpenCap project out of Stanford's Human Performance Lab — using computer vision to pull biomechanical data out of markerless motion capture and turn it into coaching insight.

To chase that, I founded a student startup club at my university and led it as president, where I designed **'Spohelp'**, a **phone-based markerless motion capture analysis solution**. The idea placed 3rd (Excellence Award) at a national university SW startup ideathon. But turning the idea into a real service ran straight into a technical wall. The precise keypoint tracking at the heart of the solution needs more than a deep understanding of computer vision and deep learning models — it needs **experience putting that technology into a service**.

More recently I've become fascinated by WestSide Barbell's **Canjugate System**, and I'm planning a project to make that training system far easier for people to reach.

There is exactly one thing I want out of this bootcamp.

> **Build a deep understanding of AI, then get real experience applying it in a service — and fold that experience back into a service of my own.**

## 🚀 Three Projects

Each project is written up as **situation → task → what I'll do → what I'll measure**. The measurement rows get replaced with real numbers once the project ships.

### STEP 1 · Pill Recognition Model (Beginner)

- **Situation** — my first end-to-end classification model on a real image dataset.
- **Task** — design an AI model that identifies drug information from a pill image and **submit it to Kaggle**.
- **What I'll do** — preprocessing and augmentation, model selection and training, hyperparameter tuning, iterative leaderboard submissions.

### STEP 2 · Conversational Search AI (Intermediate)

- **Situation** — running a system that answers from source documents myself.
- **Task** — build a document-grounded conversational search AI and tune it while **working out the trade-off between retrieval quality and cost**.
- **What I'll do** — compare chunking and embedding strategies, set up a vector DB, tune the retriever, choose prompts and models, build a response-quality evaluation pipeline.

### STEP 3 · Brand Ad Generation AI (Advanced)

- **Situation** — the final block. Turning a model **into a service**.
- **Task** — build an AI that generates brand-specific advertising and **deploy it to the cloud**.
- **What I'll do** — fine-tune a generative model, optimize it (ONNX · quantization · `vLLM`), serve with `FastAPI`, containerize with `Docker`, deploy on `GCP`.
